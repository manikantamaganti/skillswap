How SkillSwap Works

Master new skills by trading your own knowledge in four simple steps.

👤

1. Create Profile

Register with your college details, department, and academic year.

💡

2. Add Skills

List the skills you can teach and specify what you want to learn.

🤝

3. Connect & Chat

Find matched peers, send exchange requests, and chat in real time.

📅

4. Host Sessions

Schedule learning sessions with meeting links to study together.
